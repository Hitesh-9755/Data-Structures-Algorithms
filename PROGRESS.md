## Tips for Progress Tracking

1. ✅ Update immediately after solving a problem
2. ✅ Include problem source (LeetCode ID, HackerRank name, etc.)
3. ✅ Note the approach used (Brute Force, Optimized, DP, Greedy, etc.)
4. ✅ Record actual time and space complexity
5. ✅ Add brief notes about key insights or learnings
6. ✅ Review weekly to identify patterns and weak areas
